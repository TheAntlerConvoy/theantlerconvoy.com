Sold Sorter for eBay

Sort any eBay search or category page by number sold, with one click, and see how many of each item have sold right on the listing.

Live on the Chrome Web Store · coming to Edge
Add to Chrome Sold Sorter's website Privacy policy

Why it exists

eBay has no "sort by most sold"

Real numbers

eBay's search page often shows "111+ sold" for a listing that has sold more than 17,000, and no count at all for many that sell well. Sold Sorter reads the listing page for the full total, so the order is based on real numbers.

Built to keep working

It's tested against saved copies of real eBay pages from every supported country. If eBay changes its pages and Sold Sorter can't read them, it tells you instead of showing a wrong order.

Gentle with eBay

It reads listing pages two at a time with a pause between them, remembers totals for 12 hours, and stops right away if eBay asks it to slow down.

Private

It runs only on eBay search and category pages, plus ExtensionPay for payments. No server, no tracking and no ads: everything happens in your browser.
