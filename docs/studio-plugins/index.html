Old-Growth Forest Generator · $7.99

A whole forest in one click

Follows the ground

Trees follow the terrain or your baseplate and stay off steep slopes, water and cliff edges. Mark buildings and roads as exclusion zones to keep them clear.

Old-growth trees, spruces, birches and snags

With ferns, flowers, mushrooms, logs, stumps and rocks underneath. Four species mixes and four seasons.

The same seed, the same forest

A seed always rebuilds the same forest, and a Detail toggle cuts the part count roughly in half. Every forest is one undo step, and Clear All Generated removes only what the plugin placed.

Cozy Village Kit · $4.99

12 farm-village props that match

One click per prop

A farmhouse, a red barn, a windmill, a stone well, a market stall, a picket fence, a planter box, hay bales, a lantern post, a signpost, a chicken coop and a grain silo, all in one color palette.

Ready to use

Each prop is its own Model with a PrimaryPart set, and every build is one undo step. Build One Of Everything lays out all 12 in a grid.

Small touches

Fence segments line up into one long fence, the signpost text is a normal TextLabel, the lantern has a working light, and the windmill's sails are tagged for your own spin script. It shares the Forest Generator's style, so a village and its woods look like one place.
