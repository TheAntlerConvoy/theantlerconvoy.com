Billing terms

Prices, renewals, cancelling and refunds

For the Pro plans of Sold Sorter for eBay, FlipComp and Shopkeeper for Roblox.

Who handles payments

Payments go through ExtensionPay, and cards are processed by Stripe. You pay on their pages, not inside the extension, and we never see your card number. Prices are in US dollars, and checkout shows the full amount before you pay.

Plans and prices

  • Sold Sorter for eBay Pro: $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year.
  • FlipComp Pro: $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year. We are testing prices with new users, who see either $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year, or $14.99 a month or $119.99 a year. FlipComp shows your price before you pay, and that is the price you are charged.
  • Shopkeeper for Roblox Pro: $4.99 a month, or $24.99 once.

Free trials

Sold Sorter has a 7-day free trial and Shopkeeper for Roblox a 3-day one. Starting a trial asks for your email address but no card, and the trial ends by itself: nothing is charged unless you choose a plan. FlipComp has no trial; its 10 free lookups let you try it.

Renewals

Monthly plans renew every month and yearly plans every year, automatically, until you cancel. Each renewal is charged to the card you paid with, at the price you signed up at, and Stripe emails you a receipt. If we ever change the price of your plan, we will email you at least 30 days before the new price applies, and you can cancel before then. A one-time purchase (Shopkeeper's $24.99 once) never renews.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time. In Sold Sorter or FlipComp, open the extension's Settings page or its toolbar button and click Manage subscription. For any of the three, you can also email support@theantlerconvoy.com from the address you paid with, and we will cancel it for you within two business days. After you cancel, Pro keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

If you're not happy, email support@theantlerconvoy.com within 7 days of a charge, from the address you paid with, and we will refund that charge in full. That includes renewals. We also refund double charges and billing mistakes whenever they happen. Refunds go back to the card you paid with; your bank may take 5 to 10 days to show them.

Price tests

Now and then we try different prices with new users. Your price never changes because of a test. If a test ends with a lower price than the one you paid, email us and we will refund the difference.

Questions

Email support@theantlerconvoy.com. A person reads every message.
