How it works

One click on any listing

The numbers that matter

Click "Check eBay sold prices" and in a few seconds FlipComp shows the median sold price, the typical range, how many have sold, your profit after eBay fees and shipping, and the highest price that's still a good deal.

Vague titles, handled

Marketplace titles like "PS5 stuff" or "Dewalt tools" are turned into a clean eBay search by AI that reads the title, the description and, when needed, the photo. Fixing the search doesn't use another lookup.

Honest comps

It leaves out partial matches, sales marked for parts, best-offer sales where eBay hides the price paid, and prices far from the rest. Every fee, shipping cost and deal threshold can be changed in Settings.

Private

It only reads Marketplace listing pages, never profiles or messages. eBay pages load in your own browser, and there are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.
